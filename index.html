The dog who disturbed no one

In loving memory · A story from Hyderabad, Sindh

For four years, Rabeet Naeem Khanzada fed and watched over a gentle dog near her home, the calmest she had ever known. When she returned from a trip to Karachi in September, her dog had been poisoned.

She never disturbed anyone. She never harmed anyone. She lived quietly, and she was loved.“Indeed, we belong to Allah, and indeed, to Him we shall return.” Qur’an 2:156

Rabeet has never been a silent witness. She raises her voice for animal rights, and for the dog who can no longer speak. A true animal activist. A true human being.

Our agenda: helping animals

Everything we do returns to one simple agenda. This is what we ask of ourselves, and what we invite you to join.

  1. Feed and shelter strays in your own street, every single day.
  2. Rescue and treat injured or sick animals without delay.
  3. Speak up and report cruelty whenever you witness it.
  4. Host or attend awareness sessions in your community.
  5. Teach children early that mercy to animals is mercy to all.
  6. Treat this as an act of faith, not a favor to be praised for.

Looking ahead

We hope for more feeding points, more rescue capacity, more awareness sessions, and more young people who choose compassion as a habit, not an event.

We dream of a day when no dog shivers unnoticed outside a building full of people, and every one of those people has somewhere to turn.
